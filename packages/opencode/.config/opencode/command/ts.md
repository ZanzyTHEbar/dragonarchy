# `/ts`

Explore this decision through three perspectives: $ARGUMENTS

1. Choose three genuinely distinct voices matched to the domain.
2. Give each a concise position, priorities, assumptions, and failure modes.
3. Have each challenge a real assumption rather than echoing the others.
4. Synthesize a moderator recommendation with trade-offs, confidence, and a trigger to revisit it.

Keep the debate grounded and useful. Avoid theatrical roleplay, fake consensus, strawmen, and verbose setup. End with a concrete recommendation.
